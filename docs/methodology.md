# Methodology and provenance

This repository is a research workspace. The historical activities described in the project background come from the author's recollection, not verified replication of confidential employer data.

## Evidence classes

1. **Historical source**: a verifiable archived publication, document, dataset, or artifact.
2. **Measured**: new experiment with hardware/software version, seed, controls and raw outputs.
3. **Synthetic**: intentionally generated examples used for teaching and scaffolding.

The demo currently uses **synthetic** values. No historical production model, coefficients, customers, or published results are claimed.

## Reproducibility

Record exact datasets and licences, random seeds, software and hardware versions, assumptions, baseline comparisons, accuracy metrics, uncertainty, failures and limitations. Do not publish private employer or personal customer information.
