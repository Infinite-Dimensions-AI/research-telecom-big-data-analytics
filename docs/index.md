## Research question

How do operational and social relationships improve prediction and intervention decisions in historical telecom data?

## Approach

- Historical or baseline methods: Logistic regression, random forests, survival modelling, centrality metrics, association rules, Hadoop, MapReduce and early Spark.
- Modern comparison: Temporal graph neural networks, transformers, causal uplift analysis and accountable decision policies.
- Research results will be reported only after reproducible experimentation and validation.
